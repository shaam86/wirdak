## أوامر توليد الفيديو (لصق مشهد مشهد)

استخدم كل أمر مع **صورة المشهد كإطار أول (Image-to-Video)**.  
Kling / Runway / Luma: الصق النص الإنجليزي فقط. Duration حسب الجدول.

### Master negative (ألصقه مع كل مشهد)

```
Do NOT generate any Arabic text, English slogans, captions, titles, watermarks, logos of banks, or random letters. No distorted typography. Keep the frame clean for later caption overlay. Do not invent company names or fake financial numbers. Do not change the Safir app UI if it appears on screen. Photoreal, premium FinTech, cinematic, not a consumer-app ad, not a game trailer.
```

### Scene 1 — Chaos (0–6s) — start frame: `safir-video-01-chaos.png`

```
Slow cinematic camera push-in through a money-transfer and currency-exchange office. A stressed Middle-Eastern manager sits at a cluttered desk with papers, receipts, calculator, mixed banknotes, and multiple messy monitors. Warm practical lamps, deep navy shadows, shallow depth of field. Papers slightly shift, phone lights up, he looks between screens. Tense, quiet, premium corporate. 6 seconds. No text.
```

### Scene 2 — Transform (6–10s) — start: `safir-video-02-transform.png`

```
The cluttered papers and data freeze, then dissolve into cyan-blue particles. A single blue light point in the center expands and forms the Safir Accounting app icon (rounded square, white dollar, rising bars, upward arrow). Light ribbons of organized data flow into the logo. Powerful but smooth cinematic transition. 4 seconds. No text.
```

### Scene 3 — Safir Center (10–16s) — start: `safir-video-03-center.png`

```
Reveal a premium dark laptop as the control center. Keep the real Safir dashboard on the screen unchanged. A smartphone beside it shows the app. Slow orbiting camera. 3D objects (remittance slip, coins, chart, report, airplane, passport) float and connect to the laptop with thin cyan data lines flowing INTO the screen. Parallax, glass reflections. 6 seconds. No new text overlays.
```

### Scene 4 — Transfers (16–21s) — start: `safir-video-04-transfers.png`

```
Smooth macro zoom into the laptop screen showing the real Safir account statement. Cyan arrows animate a transfer journey around the bezel, then data streams into the dashboard. Fast, precise, professional. 5 seconds. No captions.
```

### Scene 5 — Currencies (21–26s) — start: `safir-video-05-currencies.png`

```
The remittance card morphs into a spinning USD coin, then other coins appear: EUR, AED, SAR, TRY, GBP. They orbit the laptop and stream into the real Safir exchange-rate dashboard. Subtle financial chart motion. Coins stay secondary to the screen. 5 seconds. No captions.
```

### Scene 6 — Flights & Visas (26–30s) — start: `safir-video-06-flights.png`

```
A coin morphs into a 3D passenger aircraft flying toward the laptop. Boarding pass and passport appear, then fold into the real Safir flight-tracking screen. Status dots pulse. 4 seconds. No captions.
```

### Scene 7 — Reports & Security (30–35s) — start: `safir-video-07-reports-security.png`

```
The flight ticket morphs into a report document. PDF and Excel icons snap into place, then a calm cyan glass shield expands around the laptop. Soft permission/user glyphs. Professional security, not scary. 5 seconds. No captions.
```

### Scene 8 — Finale (35–45s) — start: `safir-video-08-finale.png`

```
All orbiting elements collapse as cyan light into the large Safir app icon at center. Slow heroic push-in, light bloom, hold 2 seconds on the logo with empty space above and below for captions and Microsoft Store badge. Triumphant, trustworthy. 8–10 seconds. No generated text.
```
